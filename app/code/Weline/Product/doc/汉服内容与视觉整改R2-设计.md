# Hanfu Content and Visual Remediation Design

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Status:** Approved by user
**Scope:** 320 bilingual blog records, 28 product-category visuals and banners, three homepage hero banners, three Hanfu product detail records, FileManager registration and zero-reference cleanup.

## 1. Outcome

Replace generic, mismatched and repeated Hanfu content with a release-ready bilingual editorial and commerce system. Every visual must represent the named garment or craft, every factual claim must be traceable or explicitly qualified, and every new/replacement image must be selectable in FileManager with reviewed Chinese and English metadata.

## 2. Editorial model

- Preserve 160 Chinese and 160 English published records as 160 bilingual topic pairs.
- Keep existing category membership, but remove mechanical “补充说明 / Additional note” padding, repeated sales paragraphs and category SVGs embedded as article photography.
- Each article pair shares one subject and may share the same master cover. Unrelated topic pairs must not share a cover.
- Core Hanfu articles use form-first sections: identification, construction, fabric/craft, occasion, fit/purchase checks, care and source boundary.
- Marketplace/store articles are evergreen evaluation guides. They do not publish unverified availability, ranking, price, shipping or return claims.
- Ethnic-dress articles preserve cultural separation from Hanfu, qualify regional/subgroup variation, distinguish daily dress from ceremonial dress, and require source metadata for images and claims.
- Chinese and English are edited as native-language versions rather than sentence-by-sentence machine translations.
- Acceptance rejects numbered filler paragraphs, duplicated normalized paragraphs across unrelated posts, mismatched cover subjects and unqualified commercial claims.

## 3. Visual system

Direction: restrained Song–Ming elegance with premium product photography and museum-catalog precision.

- Background: warm ivory, pale ink green or deep charcoal; soft directional natural light.
- Accents: restrained cinnabar and muted gold; no fantasy glow, armor, qipao, kimono or generic “ancient costume.”
- Product category master: one unique 3:2 source per category, composed for both a 1:1 crop and a 5:1 banner crop.
- Category crop: WebP/JPEG, 800×800 target, exact garment construction visible.
- Category banner: WebP/JPEG, 1500×300 target, subject in safe zone, no baked-in copy.
- Homepage hero: 1920×600-equivalent 16:5 source, real product retained on right, localized text safe zone on left, no baked-in copy.
- Blog cover: 160 unique topic masters for 160 bilingual pairs; 16:9 output with descriptive caption and source record.
- Generated images are editorial reconstructions and recorded as such. Open museum/Commons images retain object/source/license/creator data even where attribution is optional.

## 4. Form-review rubric

Each visual is approved only when its named identifiers are visible:

- yuanling: continuous round collar;
- jiaoling/zhishen: crossing collar with right closure;
- duijin/beizi: parallel open front edges;
- qixiong/qiyao: tie and waist placement at the named height;
- mamian: flat front/back panels and side pleats;
- lanshan: round-neck robe with lower horizontal lan section;
- aoqun: upper ao and skirt relationship is legible;
- accessories: object type and wearing/fastening relation are clear.

## 5. FileManager contract

All new assets enter through `FileAssetLibraryInterface::upload`; the importer never writes FileAsset tables directly.

For every asset:

- FileAsset metadata: `project=hanfu-r2`, `asset_role`, source type, source URL/object ID, institution/creator, license name/URL, generated prompt or derivative lineage, checksum, linked module/entity IDs/slugs/locales, crop/safe-zone data and review note.
- `zh_Hans_CN` FileAssetLocale: non-empty display_name, default_alt, description and default_caption; translation_state=reviewed, translation_origin=manual.
- `en_US` FileAssetLocale: the same required fields and reviewed/manual state.
- `saveMetadata` uses the latest asset revision after upload and again after each locale update.
- Category and homepage configuration stores the public FileManager URL/object key returned by the library, not an unmanaged filesystem path.

## 6. Product-copy boundary

Only the existing catalog record may establish fiber composition, color, size range, option contents and listing craft labels. Copy may explain how to evaluate those facts, but must not invent thread count, handmade percentage, historical provenance, fit measurements, wash label or included accessories.

## 7. Migration and cleanup

1. Generate/download and visually review source assets.
2. Derive responsive crops.
3. Upload all derivatives through FileManager and save both locales.
4. Update blog, category, product and homepage references.
5. Run data, HTTP and browser acceptance.
6. Inventory references in business tables, templates/configuration, FileAsset and FileAssetLocale.
7. Delete only exact old files with a zero-reference result. Referenced product photography remains.
8. Record removed paths and retained exceptions.

## 8. Acceptance

- 320 published posts; 160 per locale; zero filler markers and zero repeated normalized filler paragraphs.
- 160 bilingual topic pairs resolve to 160 unique cover assets; cross-language sharing is intentional and documented.
- 28 category images and 28 banners pass the form rubric.
- Three localized homepage heroes render without baked-in text.
- Three products show accurate Chinese/English content and documented option boundaries.
- Every new visual has FileAsset plus reviewed/manual zh_Hans_CN and en_US locale rows with all required fields.
- Old files are absent only after zero-reference evidence.
- Real WLS browser acceptance passes homepage, category, representative blog and all three product pages in Chinese and English at 375, 768 and 1024 CSS pixels.
